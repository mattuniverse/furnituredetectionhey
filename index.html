How it works

Three steps from a blank space to a furnished 3D room you can explore.

01
🏘️

Choose a room type

Pick from living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, office or a fully custom shape and measurements that fit your space.

02
🧠

AI detects your furniture

TensorFlow.js runs COCO-SSD right in the browser, recognizing furniture in your photo and suggesting placement for each piece.

03
🌏

Customize in 2D, live in 3D

Drag furniture around your Fabric.js plan while a linked Three.js view renders every change in rich 3D. Export when it’s perfect.
